About Us

Unfiltered Space

Unfiltered Space, envisioned in January 2025 amidst the serene landscapes of Malabar, is a sanctuary of understanding and self-expression. Founded by a passionate team of youths and professionals, we are devoted to nurturing a confidential, judgment-free space where emotions find a voice, minds find clarity, and individuals find solace in genuine connection.We believe in the power of:

Community - Our community is a safe space built on empathy, trust, and genuine support—because healing happens together.

Self-Expression - Share your thoughts, emotions, and creativity freely—without fear or judgment. This is your space to express and be truly heard.

Healing & Growth - Through reflection, open sharing, and supportive guidance from those who care.

Our Services

Be part of the change. Let’s grow together!

Listeners Corner

Listeners Corner is a safe, non-judgmental space where you’re truly heard. Whether you're going through something heavy or just need someone to talk to, our trained listeners are here to offer support, empathy, and understanding—no advice, no pressure, just presence.

Online Communities & Forums

Designed to connect you with the right people—those who truly understand and respect your journey. Through group discussions and shared experiences, you’ll find support, encouragement, and healing in a safe, anonymous, and non-judgmental space. Grow together, guided by empathy and genuine connection

Acceptance Matters

Created to foster healing and personal growth, it offers virtual therapy, counselling, soft skills training, open podcasts, and community-based activities—empowering individuals to embrace self-acceptance and move forward with confidence and compassion.
